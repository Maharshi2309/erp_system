| D-25 | 18 Oct 2026 | The owner sees the setup home at `/overview` until the organization has employees or they press "Open full dashboard"; HR admins always get the dashboard. Supersedes the Phase 3 part of D-19. | One route, two stages of the same journey |
| D-26 | 18 Oct 2026 | Branch head counts are counted from employee records (inactive included, terminated excluded), not stored on the branch. | One source of truth; the "move employees first" rule uses real numbers |
| D-27 | 18 Oct 2026 | Data scope is applied when employees are loaded for a screen: organization roles see everyone, branch roles their branches, leave approvers their team, employees themselves. Out-of-scope profiles show "This person isn't here". | PRD §3.1; the backend must enforce the same rule |
| D-28 | 18 Oct 2026 | One validator (`validateEmployee`) is used by the add form, the edit form and the import, so rules can't drift apart. A work email is required only when an invitation is sent. Branch managers and HR admins may report straight to the owner. | PRD §6.3, §18 |
| D-29 | 18 Oct 2026 | Spreadsheets use the `excel` package (read .xlsx, write template/error report/export) and a built-in CSV parser. Downloads go through a small `FileDownloads` interface (browser blob download on web, faked in tests). | Pure Dart, works on web; no server needed |
| D-30 | 18 Oct 2026 | Import validation never guesses ambiguous dates: if any value could be read two ways, the person must pick DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY before validating. Fuzzy branch and manager matches are warnings, not silent fixes. | PRD §6.4 |
| D-31 | 18 Oct 2026 | Import duplicates are matched by employee ID. "Update existing" needs the Edit employees permission and an explicit acknowledgement; it changes only mapped, non-empty columns and never branch, manager or joining date. Rows are saved one by one and re-checked at save time, so a row taken meanwhile is listed as Failed. | Boards S49–S51 |
| D-32 | 18 Oct 2026 | Terminate/archive (Q-12 option b) is a dialog with effective date and reason; terminated people leave the default directory list and head counts but keep their history. Someone who still manages people can't be deactivated or terminated until their reports are reassigned. | PRD §6.3 |
| D-33 | 18 Oct 2026 | A branch move in the edit form records the effective date in the history but applies immediately (no scheduled changes without a backend). | Demo limitation, listed in the Phase 4 report |
| D-34 | 19 Oct 2026 | Punches are stored as UTC instants and grouped into work days in the branch's IANA time zone (`timezone` package, 10-year data). The app clock is a UTC instant; screens convert it to organization or branch wall time. | PRD §5.1, §7; correct day boundaries for branches in other zones |
| D-35 | 19 Oct 2026 | A session belongs to the day its shift started: on a night shift, punches before noon count for the previous day. A punch-out more than 20 h after the open punch-in starts a new (missing punch-in) session. | Overnight shifts (PRD §7.2) |
| D-36 | 19 Oct 2026 | Worked time = closed sessions minus recorded breaks; gaps between sessions are unpaid breaks. Expected = shift length minus the 60-minute allowed break. Status order: missing punch → half day (late beyond the half-day limit or ≤ half the expected time) → left early → short shift (> 1 h short) → late (after grace) → present. | Boards S53, S86–S88; branch attendance rules from Phase 3 |
| D-37 | 19 Oct 2026 | Original punches are never edited. Corrections add events (or replace one by reference) and count only when approved. A correction by the employee's own manager waits for a second person (routed to HR); nobody approves their own correction. | Board S55, PRD §7.4 audit rule |
| D-38 | 19 Oct 2026 | A punch is shown only after the repository confirms it; a retry reuses the same request id so it can't be recorded twice, and a second punch-in while a session is open is refused. | Board S86 |
| D-39 | 19 Oct 2026 | Employee correction requests (Q-12 option b): which punch, proposed time and reason, within 3 days of the day, one open request per day. Missing punches older than 3 days are "overdue". | Boards S54, S87–S88 |
| D-40 | 19 Oct 2026 | Demo attendance is generated for the last 30 days relative to "now" with the design's stories on yesterday; three Surat machine operators work the night shift. Hetal, Jignesh and Mitesh's demo accounts are now linked to their employee records. | Realistic demo that matches boards S52–S56 |
| D-41 | 19 Oct 2026 | Owners and HR admins get the attendance pages as tabs under one "Attendance" menu item; attendance managers also have them in the sidebar (board S52). | One area, two menus |
| D-42 | 19 Oct 2026 | Leave types live at organization level (board S58); a branch's Phase 3 leave setup can switch a type off or override its yearly allowance. Balances are never stored: granted (prorated upfront, or accrued on the 1st of each month after the waiting months) + carried + adjustments − approved − pending. | Boards S58, S60, S90, S92; PRD §8 |
| D-43 | 19 Oct 2026 | Request days are counted from the work calendar each time (weekly offs and holidays skipped unless the type counts them), so a holiday added later gives approved leave days back automatically. | Board S64 "1 day is returned to each" |
| D-44 | 19 Oct 2026 | Approval: step 1 is the reporting manager (HR when the branch approves centrally or there is no manager); an optional step 2 comes from the most specific policy route (type > branch > default). Nobody is routed to, or can decide, their own request; such a step goes to HR. HR never decides the same request twice. | Boards S57, S59; PRD §8.3 |
| D-45 | 19 Oct 2026 | Who decides: the owner and HR admins any pending step except their own requests; a branch manager any request from their branches (board S57 shows this); a leave approver only steps assigned to them. Who sees: owner/HR everything, branch managers their branches, leave approvers their reports. | Boards S34, S57; PRD §3.1 scopes |
| D-46 | 19 Oct 2026 | Q-12 option b for leave: "Request changes" sends a request back with a note; the employee changes dates (sent for approval again, earlier dates kept as "Changed from") or withdraws. Rejections need a reason of at least 3 characters. Approved leave can be cancelled only before it starts, and only while the policy allows it. | Boards S57, S91 |
| D-47 | 19 Oct 2026 | Holidays: an organization calendar (all branches, selected branches, optional, single day or range up to 14 days) sits on top of each branch's own list from setup. Past holidays can't be added, edited or deleted because attendance already used them. | Boards S62–S65 |
| D-48 | 19 Oct 2026 | Optional holidays are taken with an "Optional holiday" leave type that only counts optional holidays, limited per year by the policy (default 1). | Boards S63, S93 |
| D-49 | 19 Oct 2026 | Schedules are dated assignments at four levels; the most specific in effect wins (employee › shift group › branch › organization), and a person's own weekly off beats group and branch assignments. Changes can't start in the past. Attendance and leave both read the same work calendar. | Board S66 |
| D-50 | 19 Oct 2026 | Approved full-day leave turns a scheduled day without punches into "On leave" (LV) in attendance; it is not counted as absent. If someone punches on a leave day, the punches win. | Boards S33, S52, S56 |
| D-51 | 19 Oct 2026 | Leave-type and policy rules (allowance ranges, backdating ≤ 30 days, one route per scope, a default route, blackout dates) are domain validators shared by the forms and the repository. | Boards S58–S59 |
| D-52 | 19 Oct 2026 | Demo data: leave is seeded relative to "today" with the design's stories; past approved leave lands on days the attendance demo leaves absent, and the attendance demo doesn't punch on approved full days of leave. The demo calendar holds the design's 14 holidays for 2026. | Realistic demo that matches boards S34, S57–S66, S89–S93 |
| D-53 | 20 Oct 2026 | Session-refresh policy: the member's current role definition and assignment are layered over the signed-in session (`applyAccess`). A permission or role change reaches open sessions on their next rebuild: menus, guards and buttons re-check at once, and pages the member can no longer open redirect to "Access denied". A backend must also invalidate tokens server-side. | Board S68 "Applies immediately", S70 "next page load"; Phase 7 brief |
| D-54 | 20 Oct 2026 | Owner, HR Admin, Leave Approver and Employee are locked system roles; Branch Manager and Attendance Manager are seeded custom roles (board S67). A custom role "works like" one system role, which sets its menu, home page and record behaviour; its matrix sets what it may do. Owner-only cells (create/deactivate organization, create/edit/deactivate roles) can't be delegated. The last owner can't lose the role. Changing someone's role also updates their employee record so leave routing stays right. | Boards S67–S70 |
| D-55 | 20 Oct 2026 | Saving a role or security settings needs the password again ("Confirm it's you"); the re-authentication is logged. | Boards S68 (related events), S83 |
| D-56 | 20 Oct 2026 | Audit entries are append-only and sealed in a checksum chain (FNV-64 in the demo; SHA-256 on a backend). Every mutation in employees, attendance, leave, holidays, schedules, organization, branches, roles, invitations, settings and reports records who, what, when, scope, reason, before/after and a masked source address. Contact details, IDs, dates of birth and IP addresses are masked when shown or exported; owners can reveal one with a reason, which is logged. | Boards S77–S78; PRD §12 |
| D-57 | 20 Oct 2026 | Notifications are generated from the same mutations and filtered by the organization's preference matrix (event × employee/manager/HR × in-app/email). In-app messages are delivered; emails are queued only (no mail in the demo). Nobody is notified about their own action. | Boards S76, S82, S95 |
| D-58 | 20 Oct 2026 | Security and Data & export settings are owner-only; they use the owner-only roles permission. HR admins keep the other settings. | Boards S83–S84 |
| D-59 | 20 Oct 2026 | Export authorization: module view + export permissions, the reports export permission, the Data & export right for the area, and for restricted columns (mobile, personal email) both the restricted right and organization scope. Rows are limited to the people the member may see. Every export and every download is logged. | Boards S73–S75, S84; Phase 7 brief |
| D-60 | 20 Oct 2026 | Invitations sit under Roles but belong to whoever may invite people (`employees.create`), so HR admins and branch managers can resend, fix the address or revoke. Links last the security setting's invitation days. | Boards S71, S76 |
| D-61 | 20 Oct 2026 | Settings pages store only what the app uses: attendance policy edits each branch's rules used by attendance; two-step policy is checked at sign-in; invitation lifetime drives Invitations; export rights, date format, durations and CSV separator drive reports.
